How it's calculated

Data

Per-second records pulled from the strap's own memory over Bluetooth: heart rate, beat-to-beat (R-R) intervals, wrist orientation, skin temperature. Nothing leaves this phone.

HRV

RMSSD of clean R-R intervals (300–2000 ms, <20% change between beats) in 5-minute windows during sleep; nightly value = median window.

Resting HR

Lowest 10-minute average heart rate during sleep.

Sleep

Main sleep window from heart rate (longest period well below your awake level, ≥3 h); minutes with wrist movement are counted as awake. No sleep stages.

Recovery

This app's formula on WHOOP's inputs: today's HRV and resting HR vs your previous 30 days (z-scores), 0.65·HRV − 0.35·RHR, mapped to 1–99%. Needs 4 nights to calibrate. Not WHOOP's proprietary score.

Strain

Edwards TRIMP (minutes per heart-rate zone × 1…5), mapped to 0–21 as 21·(1 − e^(−TRIMP/200)).

Calories

BMR (Mifflin–St Jeor) at rest; Keytel 2005 heart-rate equation above 30% heart-rate reserve. Roughly ±10–20%.

VO₂max

Uth 2004: 15.3 × max HR ÷ resting HR. Population estimate.

Skin temp

Deviation from your 30-day baseline during sleep. Relative trend, not a medical temperature.

Activity count

The strap's own on-board movement counter (likely steps; not yet validated against a known step count).
